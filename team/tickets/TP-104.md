# TP-104: Ask before deleting a checklist or test case

**Type:** Story · **Owner:** Mei (FE) · **Points:** 2 · **Reviewer:** Jonas

## Story
As a tester, I want TestPal to confirm before it deletes a checklist or a test case, so that one misclick doesn't throw away my work and results.

## Acceptance criteria (DRAFT: finish in `/refinement TP-104`)
- Given I click delete on a checklist, when the confirmation appears, then it names the checklist and how many test cases it holds, and nothing is deleted until I confirm.
- Given the confirmation is showing, when I cancel, then nothing is deleted and the page is as it was.
- Given I confirm, when the delete finishes, then the item is gone and stays gone after a reload.
- _Open: Do we confirm every test case delete, or only cases that already have a result or note?_
- _Open: Does Escape or clicking outside the confirmation count as cancel?_
